# Zeke’s logo refresh

The owner's yellow classic car, checkered road and ZEKE’S lettering are the identity anchors. The website uses a transparent car symbol alongside live lettering, allowing white lettering on dark headers and navy lettering on light surfaces. The service line is “CAR RENTAL & TOUR SERVICES”. Existing UI action colors remain part of the application's design system.

## Deliverables

- `public/brand/zeke-logo-refined.png`: standalone transparent horizontal logo.
- `public/brand/zeke-car-mark.png`: simplified transparent car symbol used in the website.
- `output/brandkit/zeke-brand-board.png`: 3 × 3 identity presentation.
- `components/brand/zeke-logo.tsx`: responsive website lockup.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are raster assets, not vector source artwork.

## Generation prompts

### Horizontal logo

Create a single finished professional logo for ZEKE'S CAR RENTAL & TOUR SERVICES, a Cebu car rental business. Reference is the user attached black square with yellow vintage muscle car facing right in front three-quarter view, a horizontal yellow road underline tapering from a few small checkered squares at left, and widely spaced white ZEKE'S beneath. Retain the recognizable yellow classic car, right-facing three-quarter silhouette, road/checkered detail and exact brand name, but simplify linework into clean flat vector-like shapes. Wide horizontal website logo: yellow car and short road/checker line at left, ZEKE’S at right in wide geometric uppercase sans, with CAR RENTAL & TOUR SERVICES below. Dark navy lettering, yellow car, actual transparent background. No mockups, shadows, gradients or invented text.

### Compact symbol

Extract and refine only the yellow classic car with the short yellow road and checker squares from the horizontal logo. Remove all lettering. Actual transparent background. Square canvas with small margins. Simplify details for 48px web icon use: flat yellow and dark navy, thick clean contours, wheels with simple hubs, dark windows, two headlights. Preserve the right-facing front three-quarter classic coupe identity and road underline. No badge or rectangle.

### Brand board

Create a premium 3 × 3 brand-kit overview board for ZEKE’S CAR RENTAL & TOUR SERVICES, a Cebu car rental business. Repeat the yellow classic car, checkered road and geometric ZEKE’S wordmark consistently. Dark charcoal canvas, generous gutters, near-black and warm ivory panels; yellow, navy and ivory palette. Panels: primary logo; car construction; website header; Your Cebu journey.; palette; typography; matte black rental card; Cebu coastal road; compact icon and checker pattern. Sparse readable text, refined travel-service identity, no glow or fake body copy.
